3 Memories from This Year

  1. Seeing Gospel

    I really wanted to be in Gospel this year, but unfortunately (for some reason??) decided to stay in Show Choir. Throughout the year I'd peak through gospel's room and constantly talk to the people in it about the music and just experience. So when the spring gospel concert came around, I had to go! Last minute, (literally two hours before the show) I was able to sign up as an usher and found some old black clothes in my locker and met up with some ushering friends. We chatted and chilled and then got to work ushering, which by the way, was my first time. We goofed around as we did our job and got to interact with some sweet parents and supporters. Then the show started, and it was amazing!! Immediately, the vibe in the room shot into the sky, and everyone was singing and dancing. The preaching was inspiring but funny at the same time because people in the audience would randomly yell out things like Yes Lord(!), Hallelujah(!), Jesus(!), Praise the Lord(!), Tell 'em(!), Mhm(!), God Bless(!), right(!), preach(!), etc, and you can't help but giggle and yell something out too. The energy in the concert hall was electric, and my friends and I were having the time of our lives dancing and clapping out hearts out. So much so that after the show my hands were tomato red and the next morning my body was absolutely killing! We got some silly pictures and videos and it was just an amazing night.

  2. "Summertime" In Italian

    Sometimes on off days, my Italian teacher would put on "Summertime", which is an Italian teen drama about a group of friends in high school going through the normal teen drama, you know, love and relationships, partying, family issues, the works. It's hella juicy so the class would be hyped every time my teacher would put it on. We all would gossip about the characters while watching and react at things together, which was extremely funny. Can't really explain the details because I don't think it'd be appropriate, but you can imagine. We laugh and learn a lot because we'll call out Italian words we recognize and new phrases, and overall it was a nice wind-down time for the class.


  3. Show Choir Rehearsals

    Show Choir rehearsals were definitely a commitment. Waking up early, heading into the city, and spending hours working on choreo and tough repertoire wasn't always easy, but somehow we always found a way to make it fun. Working through things together ended up being a great bonding experience for the ensemble, and a lot of the time it was raining, which made the vibes so nice. Like in a movie. Even our lunch breaks were fun. Since we always had a time limit, my friends and I would literally run outside to grab food, speed-walk around and race back right as rehearsals started again. It was so cool seeing our hard work pay off as everything came together, especially in costume/mics in the concert hall rehearsals. The long rehearsals would be tiring, but they gave us some of the funniest memories and brought all of us closer together.
